## Export and deletion facts

The performed native browser CSV download contains `alias,note,enabled,mailboxes`. It is an alias-routing list, not a mail archive, complete account backup, private-key backup or history export. The native CSV import function was tested in the isolated p5 fixture with a permitted fictional custom domain and the account's recipient mailbox; the resulting row was then exported and its fields checked. This does not verify the browser upload/job path or migration of a real domain between installations. Import requires permitted domains/mailboxes and available alias addresses; deleted-address reservations can prevent reuse. The native importer reads alias, note and mailbox fields but does not restore the exported enabled flag; verify forwarding state after import. The separate **Export your data** job builds a ZIP containing user, alias, mailbox, contact, directory, domain and email-log JSON, and **emails** it to the account mailbox. Refused-message content is omitted. That mailed ZIP path was source-checked, not delivered or called tested; it still requires an authorized end-to-end delivery test through the newly verified relay connection.

EN: Download the alias CSV from the native account/settings export control and keep it private: it lists where aliases forward. It does not contain received mail. Before importing elsewhere, verify that you control the required domains and recipient mailboxes and follow that installation's import template. Account deletion is separate from deleting an alias or disabling forwarding; forwarded messages and other recipients' copies remain.

ES: Descargá el CSV de alias desde el control nativo de exportación de cuenta/ajustes y guardalo en privado: muestra los destinos de reenvío. No contiene los mensajes recibidos. Antes de importarlo en otra instalación, verificá que controlás los dominios y buzones necesarios y seguí su plantilla de importación. Borrar la cuenta es distinto de borrar un alias o detener el reenvío; los mensajes reenviados y las copias de sus destinatarios permanecen.

The native web account-deletion job attempts a confirmation email before removing the account, so it must not be advertised as verified end-to-end while its required confirmation-mail delivery remains untested.
